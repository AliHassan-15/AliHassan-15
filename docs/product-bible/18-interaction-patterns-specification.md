# Document 18 — Interaction Patterns Specification

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  
**Authority:** Implementation Specification — interaction patterns for the Engineering Operating System (EOS)  
**Nature:** Implementation-independent technical specification. Not a constitutional document. Never becomes Constitutional Authority. Never replaces Documents 00–17. Locked technical specification.  
**Depends on:** Documents 00–17 (Locked where marked Locked)  
**Implements:** Document 07 — Interaction Language; Document 15 — UI System Constitution  
**Also obeys:** Documents 00–17 (Locked where marked Locked); technical extension of Document 15 only — never Constitutional Extension or replacement of Documents 00–17  
**Answers:** HOW interaction patterns behave throughout EOS at a timeless behavioral level

## 0. Purpose

**Why.** Document 07 defines interaction philosophy. Document 15 defines UI constitutional law. Without a patterns specification, surfaces invent private interaction behavior and trust collapses.

**Specification.** This document specifies how interaction patterns behave throughout EOS. It defines interaction behavior. It applies Documents 07 and 15; it does not invent or redefine interaction philosophy. It remains implementation-independent. It never defines construction methods, libraries, inventories of interface parts, motion choreography, or visual styling. Exact named pattern inventories remain **Deferred** until governed under this specification. Deferred gaps are never filled by fashion catalogs presented as decided truth.

**Failure Conditions.** Patterns that contradict Documents 00–17. Redefinition of Interaction Language or UI Constitution. Construction-bound recipes treated as the pattern itself. Invented pattern inventories presented as locked.

---

## 1. Interaction Philosophy

**Why.** Philosophy already locked in Document 07 must not be rewritten as specification costume.

**Specification.** This section applies Document 07 Interaction Language only; it does not redefine or replace it. Interaction throughout EOS must feel like a premium engineering tool: predictable, confident, clear, responsive, intentional, mature, precise, and calm. The visitor remains in control. The visitor must never think about the interface — only the engineering. Every interaction must teach, clarify, reinforce, build confidence, or inspire exploration — or it is simplified or removed. Entertainment, manipulation, and impressive interaction that sacrifices clarity are refused. Five-year defensibility outranks temporary interaction fashion.

**Failure Conditions.** Interaction that demands attention for itself. Manipulation or entertainment as purpose. Clarity sacrificed for impressiveness. Philosophy redefined under the name of patterns.

---

## 2. Navigation Patterns

**Why.** Navigation failure destroys trust faster than visual failure. Orientation is non-negotiable.

**Specification.** Navigation patterns keep the visitor oriented at all times: where, why, next, return. Movement through the product is predictable. Narrative, structural, spatial-when-earned, and contextual modes may coexist when each remains understandable. Hidden navigation is refused. Forced pathing that strips control is refused. Experimental navigation is refused unless strongly justified and owned. Entrance invites without recreating destination depth. Destination may deepen navigation without changing identity. Same message; different depth. Exact navigation inventories and route trees remain **Deferred** (Information Architecture / later surface specs).

**Failure Conditions.** Hidden navigation. Lost orientation. Unpredictable movement. Entrance navigation that becomes destination. Surprise paths that cannot be declined. Experimental navigation without ownership.

---

## 3. Progressive Disclosure Patterns

**Why.** Forced depth is novelty. Optional depth is respect.

**Specification.** Progressive disclosure patterns deliver clear L1 first: identity, what, why. L2 deepens projects, systems, architecture, and research by visitor choice. L3 opens optional technical depth by visitor choice. Same message; different depth. Depth is optional, never forced. First visits guide and pace; return visits may deepen and move faster without changing identity. Disclosure answers an engineering question; it is not a puzzle. Exact disclosure wiring remains **Deferred** where Documents 08–10 left it Deferred; the law of optional progressive depth still binds.

**Failure Conditions.** Forced depth. Depth that cannot be declined. First-visit overload. Return visits that change identity. Optional depth made mandatory. Disclosure as entertainment or puzzle. L1 dependent on undisclosed layers.

---

## 4. Scroll Interaction

**Why.** Scroll can support understanding — or become spectacle and hijack.

**Specification.** Scroll interaction supports reading, scanning, and progressive reveal of engineering meaning. Scroll never becomes cinematic hijack. Scroll never traps the visitor or removes recoverable orientation. Scroll does not delay intentional action for atmosphere. Motion that accompanies scroll, if any, belongs to later motion specification and must not redefine this pattern: understanding outranks spectacle. Forced scrolling as primary navigation is refused.

**Failure Conditions.** Scroll hijacking. Spectacle scroll that destroys orientation. Forced scrolling as primary path. Scroll that delays understanding for atmosphere. Lost where / why / next / return under scroll.

---

## 5. Focus & Attention Patterns

**Why.** Attention earned builds trust. Attention seized is manipulation.

**Specification.** Focus follows visitor intent. Attention is earned by clarity and hierarchy — never by surprise, coercion, or artificial urgency. No surprise interaction that cannot be anticipated from context. No manipulation patterns. Ambient atmosphere never outranks interaction focus. Focus changes are understandable. Focus patterns preserve keyboard accessibility principles without prescribing construction.

**Failure Conditions.** Surprise focus shifts. Manipulative attention capture. Atmosphere competing with focus. Invisible focus. Attention patterns that reduce clarity.

---

## 6. Selection Patterns

**Why.** Unclear selection produces guesswork and false confidence.

**Specification.** Selection is visually and behaviorally clear. Active selection is distinguishable from idle state without celebration. Multi-selection, when present, remains understandable, reversible, and owned. Selection state is recoverable: the visitor can know what is selected and how to clear or change it. Selection never hides required meaning behind hover-only dependence. Exact selection inventories remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Ambiguous selection. Unrecoverable selection. Hover-only required selection meaning. Multi-selection that traps or confuses. Selection theater.

---

## 7. Input Patterns

**Why.** Input is where control is proven or lost. Ambiguous input becomes guesswork.

**Specification.** Input patterns for entry, search, filtering, and editing prioritize clarity first. Affordances are perceivable without hover-only dependence. Labels are descriptive. Input is intentional and predictable. Destructive or consequential input requires confirmation proportional to risk. Input never invents playful friction or novelty traps. Search and filter patterns preserve orientation and do not invent Discovery evidence. Exact input inventories remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Guesswork input. Invisible affordances. Hover-only required meaning. Playful friction. Missing confirmation for consequential action. Input that reduces clarity. Search/filter that invents evidence.

---

## 8. Validation Patterns

**Why.** Validation can guide — or punish. Punishment destroys calm confidence.

**Specification.** Validation feedback is honest, calm, and timely enough to prevent false confidence. Validation never punishes. Errors are recoverable. Prefer known limitation over false confidence. Validation messages explain what is wrong and what can be done next without blame theater. Validation does not invent requirements unsupported by product meaning. Exact validation catalogs remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Punitive validation. Opaque validation. Unrecoverable validation failure. False confidence preferred over known limitation. Blame theater.

---

## 9. Feedback Patterns

**Why.** Without feedback, predictability collapses. Theatrical feedback becomes entertainment.

**Specification.** Feedback is subtle, immediate, professional, consistent, and predictable. Feedback communicates state change, progress, completion, and error honestly. Ordinary actions are never celebrated. Feedback must not delay the next intentional action. Feedback must not require sound for comprehension. Feedback serves understanding and confidence — not performance.

**Failure Conditions.** Delayed feedback. Celebration of ordinary actions. Inconsistent feedback. Feedback that delays interaction. Feedback that requires sound. Opaque state change.

---

## 10. Loading Patterns

**Why.** Loading is where honesty is tested. Fake progress is deception.

**Specification.** Loading patterns communicate progress honestly. Loading is progressive where understanding benefits, without trapping the visitor. Orientation is preserved during loading: where, why, next, return remain recoverable. Fake progress is refused. Deceptive loading is refused. Ordinary loading is not celebrated. Unfinished loading is never presented as ready. Optional enhancement loading may fail closed without destroying L1 understanding.

**Failure Conditions.** Fake progress. Deceptive loading. Celebrated loading. Orientation lost during loading. Unfinished presented as ready. Optional loading collapse of core understanding.

---

## 11. Empty States

**Why.** Empty states can guide — or blame and invent absence costume.

**Specification.** Empty states explain absence honestly. Empty states guide the next meaningful action. Empty states never blame the visitor. Empty states never invent Deferred or Missing evidence as present. Empty states remain calm and professional. Exact empty-state inventories remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Blameful empty states. Invented evidence in empty states. Empty states that hide absence. Empty theater. Guidance that forces unrelated depth.

---

## 12. Error Presentation

**Why.** Error presentation is craft under stress. Costume error destroys trust.

**Specification.** Error presentation is calm, truthful, and recoverable. Errors state what failed and what can be done next. Errors do not theatricalize failure. Errors do not opaque-fail. Errors preserve visitor control and return paths. Errors do not invent false causes. Prefer known limitation over false confidence.

**Failure Conditions.** Theatrical errors. Opaque errors. Unrecoverable error paths. Blame theater. False confidence after error. Lost orientation after error.

---

## 13. Confirmation Patterns

**Why.** Confirmation protects against irreversible harm. Confirmation theater becomes friction costume.

**Specification.** Confirmation appears only when consequential risk exists. Confirmation is proportional to risk. Confirmation never becomes theater for ordinary actions. Confirmation preserves clear choice: proceed, cancel, or recover. Confirmation does not manipulate through false urgency. Exact confirmation inventories remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Confirmation theater for ordinary actions. Missing confirmation for consequential risk. Manipulative urgency. Unclear proceed/cancel. Confirmation that traps.

---

## 14. Keyboard Interaction

**Why.** Accessibility is baseline Constitutional Authority. Keyboard paths prove control without prescribing construction.

**Specification.** Keyboard interaction principles are implementation-independent. Primary paths must be operable without pointer dependence. Focus order supports understanding and orientation. Labels remain descriptive. Keyboard interaction never requires puzzle knowledge. Keyboard paths preserve the same identity and meaning as other operable paths — never a lesser product. Exact keyboard inventories remain **Deferred**; accessibility law from Documents 12 and 15 still binds.

**Failure Conditions.** Inaccessible primary paths. Pointer-only required meaning. Puzzle keyboard interaction. Keyboard path that becomes a lesser product. Missing descriptive labels.

---

## 15. Responsive Interaction

**Why.** Same identity across contexts is locked law. Density may change; understanding must not.

**Specification.** Responsive interaction preserves the same identity and the same understanding across viewing contexts. Density may differ; meaning may not. A lesser product on constrained contexts is refused. Orientation remains recoverable. Atmosphere never wins over responsiveness, accessibility, or understanding. Authoring-context-only interaction success is insufficient evidence. Exact responsive interaction inventories and breakpoint systems remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Context that becomes a lesser product. Different identity by context. Lost orientation across contexts. Atmosphere preferred over responsive understanding. Quality proven only on the authoring path.

---

## 16. Multi-step Interaction

**Why.** Multi-step flows lose trust when progress and recovery are unclear.

**Specification.** Multi-step interaction preserves orientation at every step: where in the sequence, why the step exists, what is next, and how to return. Progress is honest. Steps are recoverable where product meaning allows. Multi-step flows never trap. Multi-step flows never force unrelated depth. Multi-step flows do not celebrate ordinary step completion. Exact multi-step inventories remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Lost step orientation. Fake step progress. Unrecoverable traps. Forced unrelated depth. Celebration theater between steps.

---

## 17. Advanced Interaction

**Why.** Power-user depth must never punish first-time understanding.

**Specification.** Advanced interaction may accelerate return visits and deepen optional capability. Advanced paths must not harm first-time users. Advanced interaction remains optional. First visits prioritize guidance and pacing. Return visits may offer faster depth without changing identity. Advanced interaction never becomes the only path to L1 understanding. Exact advanced inventories remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Advanced paths required for L1. First-time harm from power-user defaults. Identity changed for return visits. Optional advanced depth made mandatory. Puzzle depth for experts that contaminates core paths.

---

## 18. State Presentation

**Why.** Document 13 binds state philosophy. Document 15 binds state presentation. Patterns must present state without lying.

**Specification.** This section applies Documents 13 and 15 to interaction-level state presentation; it does not redefine them. Loading, empty, success, error, disabled, and background conditions communicate clearly and calmly. Transient presentation state does not masquerade as durable product truth. Ordinary states are not celebrated. Unfinished state is never ready. Known limitation outranks false confidence. State transitions are understandable.

**Failure Conditions.** Unexplained state. Theatrical failure. Opaque failure. Presentation state as product truth. Celebrated ordinary states. Unfinished presented as complete. Redefinition of Documents 13 or 15.

---

## 19. Interaction Consistency

**Why.** Consistency creates trust. Inconsistent interaction language is a hard ban.

**Specification.** Comparable situations produce comparable behavior. Expectations and mental models remain stable across entrance and destination under one identity; depth differs. Feedback, disclosure, navigation orientation, and state presentation feel like one vision with Documents 07, 14, and 15. Local exceptions require owned justification and revisit conditions. Inconsistency is never personality. Same message; different depth.

**Failure Conditions.** Multiple interaction languages. Inconsistency as personality. Unowned exceptions. Silent reinterpretation of locked interaction meaning. Entrance and destination as different behavioral brands.

---

## 20. Interaction Review Standards

**Why.** Patterns without review become novelty culture. Review enforces Documents 07, 12, and 15.

**Specification.** Interaction pattern review must answer:

1. Does this obey Documents 00–18?  
2. Does it apply Documents 07 and 15 without redefining them?  
3. Does the visitor focus on engineering rather than the interaction?  
4. Is behavior predictable; is orientation recoverable (where / why / next / return)?  
5. Is progressive depth optional?  
6. Is feedback subtle, immediate, professional, and non-celebratory for ordinary actions?  
7. Are loading, empty, and error presentations honest and recoverable?  
8. Do entrance and destination keep same message with different depth?  
9. Do accessibility and performance hold as baseline?  
10. Would we still choose this in five years?  
11. Does any Document 12 interaction or accessibility quality dimension fail?

One failed required answer voids approval. Fashion is never a valid yes. Schedule pressure does not redefine Done.

**Failure Conditions.** Review that asks only whether behavior executes. Novelty accepted as craft. Silent conflict with locked documents. Approval without five-year defensibility. Done redefined by schedule.

## 21. Interaction Anti-Patterns

**Why.** Named refusals protect culture before habits harden.

**Specification.** The following are forbidden. Presence blocks acceptance until corrected:

| Forbidden behavior | Detectable when | Enforcement |
|--------------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Hidden navigation | Where / why / next / return unrecoverable | Reject |
| Surprise behavior | Behavior cannot be anticipated from context | Reject |
| Scroll hijacking | Scroll removes control or traps for spectacle | Reject |
| Forced interaction | Progress requires coerced action without decline path | Reject |
| Hover-only information | Required meaning unavailable without pointer hover | Reject |
| Puzzle interaction | Understanding requires novelty decoding | Reject |
| Manipulation | Attention or action seized by coercion or false urgency | Reject |
| Celebration of ordinary actions | Ordinary load/success/input is theatrical | Reject |
| Interaction before understanding | Interaction precedes or blocks clarity of meaning | Reject |
| Inconsistent interaction language | Comparable situations behave incompatibly without owned exception | Reject |
| Optional depth becoming mandatory | Deeper levels cannot be declined; L1 depends on depth | Reject |
| False progress | Progress communicated without truthful advancement | Reject |
| Deceptive loading | Loading misrepresents readiness or progress | Reject |
| Inaccessible interaction | Primary path inoperable without excluded capability | Reject |
| Entrance recreates destination | Entrance carries destination interaction depth | Reject |
| Third public interaction surface | Behavior invents a public product beyond entrance/destination | Reject |
| Invented evidence via interaction | Deferred/Missing presented as present through flow | Reject |
| Silent constitutional conflict | Conflict with Documents 00–18 resolved without stopping to ask | Reject |

**Failure Conditions.** Anti-patterns negotiated as taste. Temporary labels without exit. Partial credit for forbidden behaviors.

## 22. Interaction Evolution

**Why.** EOS evolves. Interaction evolution must deepen clarity without costume change.

**Specification.** Evolve by deepening predictability, strengthening orientation and accessibility, reducing unjustified interaction cost, clarifying recovery, and removing what no longer deserves to remain. Preserve same message with greater depth over time. Prefer patterns that age. Exact inventory decisions exiting Deferred require governance review. Trend reinvention is not evolution. Accumulation without reduction is refused. Temporary experiments stay labeled and isolated until they meet Document 12 acceptance discipline and this specification.

**Failure Conditions.** Constant interaction costume change. Accumulation without reduction. Silent exit from Deferred inventories. Evolution that weakens Documents 00–17 compliance.

## 23. Interaction Manifesto

The interface disappears into understanding.

Predictability outranks novelty.

Orientation is non-negotiable.

Depth is optional.

Feedback is honest and calm.

Ordinary actions are not celebrated.

Scroll serves reading, never hijack.

Attention is earned, never seized.

Accessibility is baseline.

Same message. Different depth. No costume.

If the visitor thinks about the interaction, the interaction has failed.

If it would not deserve to remain in five years, it does not deserve to enter the pattern system now.
