**Status:** Locked

## 0. Purpose

**Why.** Document 06 defines what motion is. Document 15 defines UI constitutional law. Without a motion system specification, surfaces invent private movement and understanding collapses into spectacle.

**Specification.** This document specifies how motion behaves throughout EOS while remaining completely implementation-independent. It specifies motion systems. It applies Document 06 Motion Language; it does not redefine it. It applies Document 15 UI constitutional law where motion meets interface structure. It never defines construction methods, libraries, engines, recipes, or exact animation inventories. Exact animation recipes, choreography, and motion inventories remain **Deferred** until later governed implementation specifications. Deferred gaps are never filled by fashion catalogs presented as decided truth.

**Failure Conditions.** Motion systems that contradict Documents 00–18. Redefinition of Motion Language or UI Constitution. Construction-bound recipes treated as the motion system itself. Invented animation inventories presented as locked.

## 1. Motion Philosophy

**Why.** Philosophy already locked in Document 06 must not be rewritten as specification costume.

**Specification.** This section applies Document 06 Motion Language only; it does not redefine or replace it. Motion exists to communicate understanding, strengthen continuity, and reinforce engineering clarity — never to entertain, distract, or seek attention. Emotion is not manufactured by effects. Motion explains; it does not decorate. If movement does not improve understanding or reduce cognitive load, it does not belong. The objective is never to demonstrate that movement can be built. Five-year defensibility outranks temporary motion fashion.

**Failure Conditions.** Motion that entertains or seeks attention. Decorative motion as identity. Philosophy redefined under the name of systems. Impressive movement that sacrifices clarity.

---

## 2. Motion Taxonomy

**Why.** Without taxonomy, every movement becomes a special case and consistency collapses.

**Specification.** EOS motion is classified by communicative role, not by vendor family. Categories only — never exact animations:

1. Reveal
2. Focus
3. Connect
4. Transition
5. Supportive breathe
6. State change
7. Environmental ambient
8. Hierarchical emphasis

Taxonomy classifies communicative roles for system composition and review. It applies Document 06; it does not redefine Motion Language. It does not replace Document 06 behavior priority: Reveal → Focus → Connect → Transition → Emphasize → Breathe (supportive only). A movement belongs to one primary communicative role. Taxonomy does not invent exact animation inventories. Exact named motion catalogs remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Unclassified product motion. Taxonomy used to smuggle an effect gallery. Classification by fashion rather than communicative role. Invented animation inventories presented as decided.

---

## 3. Hierarchical Motion

**Why.** Motion without hierarchy competes with information hierarchy and destroys calm.

**Specification.** Motion hierarchy follows information hierarchy. Primary motion serves primary meaning. Secondary motion serves secondary meaning. Tertiary motion serves optional or ambient support. Motion never grants equal visual importance across unequal meaning. Hierarchy before density applies to movement. Optional depth motion remains optional. Exact hierarchical motion inventories remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Equal motion importance across unequal meaning. Ambient motion competing with primary meaning. Forced tertiary motion for L1. Hierarchy inverted by movement.

---

## 4. Transition Principles

**Why.** Transitions either preserve continuity or become entertainment between states.

**Specification.** Movement preserves continuity. Movement explains change. Movement never entertains. Transitions remain predictable and calm. Stillness follows large transitions. Pause precedes new complexity. Long theatrical transitions are refused. Exact transition recipes remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Entertaining transitions. Unexplained change. Lost continuity. Theatrical or cinematic transitions. Transitions that delay understanding for atmosphere.

## 5. Spatial Continuity

**Why.** Spatial confusion destroys orientation and systems understanding.

**Specification.** Motion preserves orientation: where, why, next, return remain recoverable. Relationships between elements and systems remain maintainable through movement. Motion never creates spatial confusion for spectacle. Depth motion is earned for understanding and must be removable without destroying core meaning. Exact spatial motion inventories remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Orientation lost under motion. Relationships broken by movement. Spatial spectacle. Depth required for core understanding. Confusion preferred over clarity.

## 6. Entrance Motion

**Specification.** Entrance motion supports understanding of identity, what, and why. Entrance motion is never theatrical. Entrance motion is never cinematic-for-itself. Entrance motion is never marketing. Entrance motion does not recreate destination depth inside the entrance surface. Same message; different depth. Exact entrance motion inventories remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Theatrical entrance. Cinematic entrance. Marketing entrance. Entrance motion that becomes destination. Entrance spectacle before understanding.

## 7. Exit Motion

**Why.** Exit should release attention calmly. Dramatic exit seeks attention.

**Specification.** Exit motion is predictable and calm. Exit motion is never dramatic. Exit preserves recoverable return where product meaning requires it. Exit does not theatricalize departure. Exact exit motion inventories remain **Deferred**.

## 8. State Transition Motion

**Why.** Document 13 binds state philosophy. Document 15 binds state presentation. Document 18 applies both at interaction level. Motion may accompany state presentation; it must not redefine state law or invent durable state catalogs.

**Specification.** This section applies Documents 13 and 15 to motion that accompanies state presentation; it does not redefine them. It composes with Document 18 state presentation without replacing it. Motion may accompany loading, empty, success, error, disabled, and background conditions — including transient updating under background, and calm readiness only as honest absence of loading theater — never as newly invented durable product-state inventory. State changes remain honest. Ordinary states are not celebrated through motion. Fake progress motion is refused. Deceptive readiness motion is refused. Motion must not delay the next intentional action. Unfinished state is never presented as ready through movement. Exact state-motion inventories remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Celebratory ordinary state motion. Fake progress. Deceptive readiness. Opaque state change via motion. Motion delaying intentional action. Unfinished presented as ready. Redefinition of Documents 13 or 15. Invented durable state catalogs presented as decided.

## 11. Scroll-related Motion

**Specification.** Scroll-related motion follows content. Motion never controls the visitor. Motion never hijacks scrolling. Motion never creates spectacle from scroll. Scroll-related motion preserves orientation and does not delay intentional action for atmosphere. Forced scrolling as primary navigation remains refused under Document 18. ## 12. Motion Reduction

**Why.** Understanding must survive without motion. Motion is never the sole channel.

**Specification.** Reduced-motion paths preserve clarity, hierarchy, continuity, relationships, orientation, accessibility, and understanding. Same understanding; different movement. Motion never becomes the only communication channel. Sound is never required to replace reduced motion. Optional spatial and ambient techniques fail closed without destroying L1. Exact reduced-motion inventories remain **Deferred**; Documents 06, 12, and 15 still bind.

**Failure Conditions.** Understanding lost without motion. Motion as sole channel. Lesser product under reduced motion. Core meaning dependent on ambient or spatial motion.

## 13. Performance Principles

**Why.** Atmosphere never outranks responsiveness. Document 12 binds performance quality.

**Specification.** Every motion has a justified cost against improved understanding. Atmosphere never outranks responsiveness, accessibility, or battery-respecting real use. Prefer simpler or shorter communicative movement when clarity is equal — without prescribing measures here. Authoring-path-only motion success is insufficient evidence. Unowned performance debt from motion is refused. Exact performance budgets remain **Deferred**.

**Failure Conditions.** Unjustified motion cost. Atmosphere preferred over responsiveness. Quality proven only on the authoring path. Unowned motion performance debt.

## 14. Accessibility Principles

**Why.** Accessibility is baseline Constitutional Authority. Motion remains optional for comprehension.

**Specification.** Motion remains optional for understanding. Understanding survives without motion. Labels, structure, and contrast outrank mood. Keyboard and non-motion paths remain complete. No motion technique is kept if it cannot remain accessible without destroying its communicative purpose. Exact accessibility motion inventories remain **Deferred**; Documents 12 and 15 still bind.

**Failure Conditions.** Inaccessible primary paths due to motion. Understanding dependent on motion. Accessibility deferred as polish. Mood preferred over access.

## 15. Motion Consistency

**Why.** Consistency creates trust. Inconsistent motion language is a hard ban.

**Specification.** One motion language. One motion identity across light and dark and across entrance and destination; depth of capability differs. Comparable situations produce comparable communicative movement. Local exceptions require owned justification and revisit conditions. Fashionable deviation is not iteration. Same message; different depth.

**Failure Conditions.** Multiple motion languages. Mode or surface motion identity forks. Inconsistency as personality. Entrance and destination as different motion brands.

Motion composes with design foundations (Document 16) without inventing literals. Motion never invents a third public product surface. Exact composition inventories remain **Deferred**.

## 17. Motion Review Standards

**Why.** Motion without review becomes effect culture. Review enforces Documents 06, 12, and 15.

**Specification.** Motion system review must answer:

1. Does this obey Documents 00–19?  
2. Does it apply Document 06 without redefining it?  
3. Does movement reveal, focus, connect, transition, emphasize, or supportively breathe — not entertain?  
4. Does understanding survive without this motion?  
5. Is hierarchy preserved; does ambient never compete with interaction?  
6. Do entrance and destination keep same message with different depth?  
7. Are reduced-motion and accessibility paths complete?  
8. Is performance cost justified against real use?  
9. Would we still choose this in five years?  
10. Does any Document 12 motion, performance, or accessibility quality dimension fail?

One failed required answer voids approval. Fashion is never a valid yes. Schedule pressure does not redefine Done.

**Failure Conditions.** Review that asks only whether movement executes. Novelty accepted as craft. Silent conflict with locked documents. Approval without five-year defensibility. Done redefined by schedule.

## 19. Motion Evolution

**Why.** EOS evolves. Motion evolution must deepen clarity without costume change.

**Specification.** Evolve by deepening communicative clarity, strengthening continuity and accessibility, reducing unjustified motion cost, and removing what no longer deserves to remain. Preserve same message with greater depth over time. Prefer motion that ages. Exact inventory decisions exiting Deferred require governance review. Trend reinvention is not evolution. Accumulation without reduction is refused. Temporary experiments stay labeled and isolated until they meet Document 12 acceptance discipline and this specification.

**Failure Conditions.** Constant motion costume change. Accumulation without reduction. Silent exit from Deferred inventories. Evolution that weakens Documents 00–18 compliance.

## 20. Motion Manifesto

Motion communicates. It does not entertain.

Continuity outranks spectacle.

Hierarchy outranks ambient noise.

Stillness is part of the language.

Understanding survives without movement.

Atmosphere never outranks responsiveness.

Attention is guided, never seized.

Scroll follows content, never hijacks.

Same message. Different depth. No costume.

If removal improves clarity, the motion did not deserve to remain.

If it would not deserve to remain in five years, it does not deserve to enter the motion system now.
