## 0. Purpose

**Why.** Document 09 defines what content belongs where. Documents 05, 15, 17, 18, and 20 govern how surfaces look, structure, and behave. Without a content presentation specification, surfaces invent private presentation habits and Discovery honesty collapses into costume.

**Specification.** This document specifies how engineering content is presented throughout EOS while remaining completely implementation-independent. It governs presentation behavior only. It applies Documents 09, 05, 15, 17, 18, and 20; it does not redefine them. It composes with Document 17 without redefining the component system. It never defines content strategy ownership, visual identity, typography, color, layout, interaction ownership, component inventories, motion, construction methods, markup languages, rendering engines, or presentation inventories. Exact rendering mechanisms and presentation inventories remain **Deferred** until later governed implementation specifications. Deferred gaps are never filled by fashion catalogs presented as decided truth.

**Failure Conditions.** Presentation systems that contradict Documents 00–20. Redefinition of Content Architecture, Design Language, UI Constitution, Component System, Interaction Patterns, or Layout System. Construction-bound recipes treated as the presentation system itself. Invented presentation inventories presented as locked.

## 1. Content Presentation Philosophy

**Why.** Content ownership is locked in Document 09. Visual and UI law relevant to presentation is locked in Documents 05 and 15. Presentation philosophy must not be rewritten as specification costume.

**Specification.** This section applies Documents 09, 05, and 15 to content presentation; it does not redefine or replace them. Presentation exists so visitors understand engineering — never so surfaces perform. Presentation serves systems thinking, decisions, proof, and clarity. Luxury through restraint. Private engineering workspace calm. Same message; different depth. Entrance never becomes destination through presentation depth. Presentation never invents a third public product surface. Discovery honesty outranks completeness costume. Five-year defensibility outranks temporary presentation fashion.

**Failure Conditions.** Presentation that entertains or seeks attention. Completeness costume over honesty. Philosophy redefined under the name of presentation. Entrance presentation recreating destination. Third public surface via presentation.

## 2. Reading Flow

**Why.** Broken reading flow destroys continuity and forces attention onto the surface instead of the engineering.

**Specification.** Presented content supports sustained reading and calm scan. Primary meaning is reachable first. Secondary meaning waits. Optional depth remains optional. **Failure Conditions.** Reading flow that forces attention to the surface. Primary meaning delayed by secondary spectacle. Trapping sequences. Lost orientation under normal reading.

## 21. Review Standards

**Why.** Presentation without review becomes content costume culture. Review enforces Documents 09, 12, and 15.

**Specification.** Content presentation review must answer:

1. Does this obey Documents 00–21?  
2. Does it apply Documents 09, 05, 15, 17, 18, and 20 without redefining them?  
3. Does presentation serve engineering understanding rather than surface performance?  
4. Is Discovery honesty preserved for Empty, Missing, and Deferred content?  
5. Is progressive depth optional; is L1 clear first?  
6. Do entrance and destination keep same message with different depth?  
7. Does reading continuity and orientation remain recoverable?  
8. Do accessibility and performance hold as baseline?  
9. Would we still choose this in five years?  
10. Does any Document 12 accessibility or performance quality dimension fail?

One failed required answer voids approval. Fashion is never a valid yes. Schedule pressure does not redefine Done.

**Failure Conditions.** Review that asks only whether content appears. Novelty accepted as craft. Silent conflict with locked documents. Approval without five-year defensibility. Done redefined by schedule.

## 23. Evolution Principles

**Why.** EOS evolves. Presentation evolution must deepen clarity without costume change.

**Specification.** Evolve by deepening honesty, strengthening reading continuity, reducing unjustified presentation complexity, hardening accessibility, clarifying absence, and removing what no longer deserves to remain. Preserve same message with greater depth over time. Prefer presentation that ages. Exact inventory decisions exiting Deferred require governance review. Trend reinvention is not evolution. Accumulation without reduction is refused. Temporary experiments stay labeled and isolated until they meet Document 12 acceptance discipline and this specification.

**Failure Conditions.** Constant presentation costume change. Accumulation without reduction. Silent exit from Deferred inventories. Evolution that weakens Documents 00–20 compliance.

## 24. Presentation Manifesto

Presentation shows engineering. It does not perform.

Honesty outranks completeness costume.

Reading continuity outranks collage.

Progressive unfold outranks overload.

Same message. Different depth. No costume.

Absence remains honest.

Atmosphere never outranks responsiveness.

If the visitor thinks about the presentation, the presentation has failed.

If it would not deserve to remain in five years, it does not deserve to enter the presentation system now.
